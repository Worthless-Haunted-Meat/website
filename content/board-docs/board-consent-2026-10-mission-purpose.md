# Unanimous Written Consent of the Board of Directors

**Worthless Haunted Meat**, a Texas nonprofit corporation (the "Corporation")
Texas SOS File No. 0805483107 · EIN 99-2244505

The undersigned, constituting all of the members of the Board of Directors of the Corporation — Ricardo A. Vega Jr., Joshua Behrenbrinker and David Flesch — act without a meeting as the Corporation's Bylaws permit: "any action that may be taken at a meeting of the Board may be taken without a meeting if consent in writing setting forth the action so taken shall be signed by all Board members," and "electronic signatures or other electronic 'consent click' acknowledgments shall be effective as original signatures." The Corporation has no members, so the Board alone may amend its Certificate of Formation. Each undersigned director waives any notice that would otherwise be required for the actions below. This consent is effective when all three directors have signed it. The undersigned adopt the following resolutions:

1. **Mission Statement.** RESOLVED, that the Board adopts the following as the mission statement of the Corporation, to be pursued exclusively within the charitable and educational purposes stated in Article 5 of its Certificate of Formation and within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code:

   > *"We discover what people love — then we use technology to help them do more of it, and teach them to bring others along."*

2. **Amendment of Article 5 (Purpose).** RESOLVED, that Article 5 of the Corporation's Certificate of Formation, filed with the Texas Secretary of State on March 20, 2024, which currently reads:

   > Worthless Haunted Meat harnesses technology and community efforts to turn surplus food into a solution for homelessness, fostering recovery and unity. Our mission: empower communities, support recovery, and ensure no one is left behind.

   is amended and restated in its entirety to read as follows:

   > The Corporation is organized exclusively for charitable and educational purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended (or the corresponding section of any future federal tax code), including but not limited to:
   >
   > (a) using technology and community efforts to redistribute surplus food and other resources to persons experiencing homelessness or economic hardship;
   >
   > (b) educating and equipping underserved individuals and communities with technological skills, tools, and opportunities;
   >
   > (c) supporting persons in recovery through structured service opportunities; and
   >
   > (d) engaging in any other activities permitted to be carried on by an organization exempt from federal income tax under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.
   >
   > No part of the net earnings of the Corporation shall inure to the benefit of, or be distributable to, its directors, officers, or other private persons, except that the Corporation shall be authorized to pay reasonable compensation for services rendered and to make payments and distributions in furtherance of the purposes set forth above. Upon dissolution of the Corporation, assets shall be distributed for one or more exempt purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, or to the federal government or a state or local government for a public purpose.

   The amendment keeps the Corporation's original purpose as item (a). The Board adopts it by the unanimous vote of all directors in office, which satisfies Section 22.107 of the Texas Business Organizations Code for a corporation without members.

3. **Filing.** RESOLVED, that the President is authorized and directed to execute a Certificate of Amendment (Texas Secretary of State Form 424) reflecting the amendment in Resolution 2, to file it with the Texas Secretary of State together with the $25 filing fee, and to pay that fee from the Corporation's funds or be reimbursed for it. The amendment takes effect when the Secretary of State files the Certificate of Amendment.

4. **Notices.** RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to provide a copy of the filed Certificate of Amendment to the Internal Revenue Service and the Texas Comptroller of Public Accounts where required or helpful, including in connection with the Corporation's pending application for exemption from Texas franchise tax, and to update the Corporation's website and records to reflect the amended purpose and the mission statement.

5. **General Authority.** RESOLVED, that the officers of the Corporation are authorized to take all further action and sign all further documents they consider necessary or appropriate to carry out these resolutions.

This consent may be signed in counterparts, each of which is an original. It shall be filed in the minute book of the Corporation and has the same force and effect as a unanimous vote of the Board taken at a duly called meeting.
