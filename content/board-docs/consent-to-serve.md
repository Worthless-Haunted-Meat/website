# Consent to Serve as Director

**Worthless Haunted Meat**, a Texas nonprofit corporation (the "Corporation")

I, the undersigned, hereby consent to serve as a director of the Corporation, effective as of the date of my signature below, and acknowledge the following:

1. **Acceptance.** I accept appointment to the Board of Directors of the Corporation and agree to serve until my term expires under the Corporation's bylaws or until my earlier resignation or removal.

2. **Duties.** I understand that as a director I owe the Corporation the duties of care, loyalty, and obedience under Texas law, as summarized in the Corporation's *Board Member Risks & Responsibilities* disclosure, which I have read.

3. **Mission.** I have read the Corporation's mission statement and its Certificate of Formation, and I am willing to help govern the Corporation in service of its charitable purposes under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.

4. **Conflicts of Interest.** I agree to disclose any actual or potential conflicts of interest to the Board and to comply with the Corporation's Conflict of Interest Policy once adopted.

5. **No Compensation.** I understand that directors of the Corporation serve without compensation.

6. **Electronic Signature.** I agree that my typed name, submitted through the Corporation's board portal together with the date and time of submission, constitutes my electronic signature under the Texas Uniform Electronic Transactions Act and the federal E-SIGN Act, and has the same force and effect as a handwritten signature.
