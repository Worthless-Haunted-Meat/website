# Unanimous Written Consent of the Board of Directors in Lieu of a Meeting

**Worthless Haunted Meat**, a Texas nonprofit corporation (the "Corporation")

The undersigned, being the sole remaining director of the Corporation, acting pursuant to the Corporation's bylaws and Section 22.208 of the Texas Business Organizations Code (which permits the remaining directors to fill vacancies on the board even though less than a quorum), hereby consents to and adopts the following resolutions:
