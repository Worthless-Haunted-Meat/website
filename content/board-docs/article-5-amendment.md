# Article 5 Amendment — Purpose Clause (Reference)

This document shows the current purpose language from the Corporation's Certificate of Formation (Form 202, filed March 15, 2024) and the proposed amended and restated language the Board will consider. Filing is by Certificate of Amendment (Form 424) with the Texas Secretary of State, $25 fee.

## Current Language (as filed)

> Worthless Haunted Meat harnesses technology and community efforts to turn surplus food into a solution for homelessness, fostering recovery and unity. Our mission: empower communities, support recovery, and ensure no one is left behind.

## Proposed Amended & Restated Article 5

> The Corporation is organized exclusively for charitable and educational purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended (or the corresponding section of any future federal tax code), including but not limited to:
>
> (a) using technology and community efforts to redistribute surplus food and other resources to persons experiencing homelessness or economic hardship;
>
> (b) educating and equipping underserved individuals and communities with technological skills, tools, and opportunities;
>
> (c) supporting persons in recovery through structured service opportunities; and
>
> (d) engaging in any other activities permitted to be carried on by an organization exempt from federal income tax under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.
>
> No part of the net earnings of the Corporation shall inure to the benefit of, or be distributable to, its directors, officers, or other private persons, except that the Corporation shall be authorized to pay reasonable compensation for services rendered and to make payments and distributions in furtherance of the purposes set forth above. Upon dissolution of the Corporation, assets shall be distributed for one or more exempt purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, or to the federal government or a state or local government for a public purpose.

## Why This Change

- The current clause limits the Corporation to surplus-food redistribution for homelessness. The restated clause **preserves that original purpose** as item (a) while adding education/empowerment (b) and recovery-support (c) lanes, with (d) providing general 501(c)(3) flexibility.
- The inurement and dissolution paragraphs are the standard language the IRS expects in the formation document of a 501(c)(3).

## Filing Steps (after Board approval)

1. Board approves the restated Article 5 (resolution at the first meeting)
2. President executes Form 424 (Certificate of Amendment)
3. File with the Texas Secretary of State ($25) via SOSUpload or mail
4. Keep the file-stamped amendment with the corporate records; provide to the IRS if ever filing a full Form 990 or updating the exemption record
