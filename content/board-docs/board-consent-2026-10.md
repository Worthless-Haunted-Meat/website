# Unanimous Written Consent of the Board of Directors

**Worthless Haunted Meat**, a Texas nonprofit corporation (the "Corporation")
Texas SOS File No. 0805483107 · EIN 99-2244505

The undersigned, constituting all of the members of the Board of Directors of the Corporation — Ricardo A. Vega Jr., Joshua Behrenbrinker and David Flesch — act without a meeting as the Corporation's Bylaws permit: "any action that may be taken at a meeting of the Board may be taken without a meeting if consent in writing setting forth the action so taken shall be signed by all Board members," and "electronic signatures or other electronic 'consent click' acknowledgments shall be effective as original signatures." The Bylaws fix the Board at three directors but say that 7 persons constitute a quorum, a number the Board cannot reach; every director in office signs this consent, and Resolution 5 corrects the quorum. Each undersigned director waives any notice that would otherwise be required for the actions below. This consent is effective when all three directors have signed it. The undersigned adopt the following resolutions:

1. **Election of Officers.** RESOLVED, that the following persons are elected to the offices stated, each to serve until a successor is elected and qualified or until earlier resignation or removal:
   - Chair of the Board (Chief Executive Officer) — Ricardo A. Vega Jr.
   - President (Executive Director) — Ricardo A. Vega Jr.
   - Secretary — Joshua Behrenbrinker
   - Treasurer (Chief Financial Officer) — Joshua Behrenbrinker

   The Bylaws permit one person to hold any number of offices, except that neither the Secretary nor the Treasurer may also serve as the Chair of the Board or the President. David Flesch serves as a director without an office.

2. **Depository Account.** RESOLVED, that the Corporation is authorized to open and maintain one or more deposit accounts in its name with Credit Union of Texas, or another federally insured credit union selected by the President (the "Credit Union"), and the officers of the Corporation are authorized to apply for membership on the Corporation's behalf, sign the account agreements the Credit Union requires, and furnish the Credit Union with such organizational documents, identification and certifications as it may reasonably request. The President shall report the account to the Board in writing once it is opened, and the Secretary shall file that report with this consent. The Secretary is authorized to certify these resolutions, and the names of the Corporation's officers and authorized signers, to the Credit Union once this consent is effective.

3. **Authorized Signers.** RESOLVED, that pursuant to the Bylaws, which provide that "all checks and drafts drawn on banks or other depositories on funds to the credit of the Corporation … shall be signed by such person or persons as the Board of Directors shall authorize to do so," the Board designates the following authorized signers on the Corporation's Credit Union accounts, each with authority to sign checks and drafts, make deposits and withdrawals, initiate transfers, and otherwise transact business on those accounts for the Corporation:
   - Primary signer — Ricardo A. Vega Jr., Chair of the Board and President
   - Backup signer — Joshua Behrenbrinker, Secretary and Treasurer

   Either signer may act alone on any single check, withdrawal, transfer or other payment of $1,000 or less. Any single payment of more than $1,000, and any series of related payments that together exceed $1,000, requires the approval of both signers, given by both signatures where the Credit Union supports it, or otherwise by the second signer's written approval (email is sufficient) before the payment is made and filed with the Corporation's records. These designations remain in effect until the Board revokes or amends them and delivers written notice to the Credit Union.

4. **Oversight.** RESOLVED, that both authorized signers shall have full access to the Corporation's account statements and transaction activity, any director may request copies at any time, and the Board shall review the Corporation's financial activity at each regular meeting.

5. **Quorum.** RESOLVED, that the sentence of the Bylaws reading "At each meeting of the Board of Directors or Board Committees, the presence of 7 persons shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business" is amended to read: "At each meeting of the Board of Directors or Board Committees, the presence of a majority of the directors then in office, or of the members of the committee, shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business." The Board adopts this amendment unanimously, which exceeds the two-thirds vote the Bylaws require to amend them. The Bylaws provide for amendments at a meeting, and they also permit any action that may be taken at a meeting to be taken by the written consent of all Board members; each director waives the meeting and the advance written notice of the proposed revision that the Bylaws would otherwise require.

6. **Ratification.** RESOLVED, that all actions taken by the directors and officers of the Corporation before the date of this consent in furtherance of its organization, formation and purposes are ratified, approved and confirmed.

7. **General Authority.** RESOLVED, that the officers of the Corporation are authorized to take all further action and sign all further documents they consider necessary or appropriate to carry out these resolutions.

This consent may be signed in counterparts, each of which is an original. It shall be filed in the minute book of the Corporation and has the same force and effect as a unanimous vote of the Board taken at a duly called meeting.
