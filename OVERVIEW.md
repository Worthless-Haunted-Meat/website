## Core Programs

### 1. Community Engagement
Gather feedback and suggestions from communities through online surveys and social media campaigns — understanding their views on homelessness and how resources should be allocated.

### 2. Technology-Driven Distribution Platform (The App — Primary Build)
A mobile application that allows **restaurants and local businesses to donate excess food and resources** to shelters or directly to individuals in need.

- Volunteers (e.g., AA/NA members) can use the app to facilitate deliveries — think hybrid of **Amazon + Uber**
- Tracks company tax write-offs
- Tracks volunteer service hours
- Status: **In development**

### 3. Homeless Assistance Campaigns
Partner with local shelters and organizations to provide meals, clothing, and necessities — coordinating distribution through the tech platform.
